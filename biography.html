My Path to Software Development

I'm a CS student at FINKI focusing on AI engineering and full-stack development. My coding journey started with competitive programming through MENDO during high school, which taught me how to think through complex problems and write efficient algorithms. That competitive background gave me a solid foundation, but I realized I wanted to build things people actually use, not just solve abstract puzzles.

What I've Built

My latest project is DocuBrain, a full-stack app where users upload PDFs, chat with them and get answers with links to the source, plus tools for invoice extraction and document comparison. During my internship at Finqup I built EmailAuto, an AI email automation platform where several AI agents classify, reason about and draft replies to emails. Meanwhile I built a route optimization app with React and Django, a cryptocurrency analysis platform with FastAPI microservices, and a team task manager in C# .NET. Each project taught me something different about system design, API integration, and building applications that people can actually use.

Technical Focus

I focus on turning AI capabilities into reliable product features: agents that automate real workflows, assistants that answer from a company's own documents, and LLM output that the rest of the application can trust. Just as important is everything around the AI, from authentication and databases to tests, CI/CD and deployment. I prefer solving problems through thoughtful design rather than quick fixes.
